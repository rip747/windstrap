Crossfade #

Add .carousel-fade to your carousel to animate slides with a fade transition instead of a slide. Depending on your carousel image height, you may need to add .top-0 and .h-100 on the .carousel-item to prevent images from breaking the layout.

Dark variant #

Add .carousel-dark to the .carousel for darker controls, indicators, and captions.
