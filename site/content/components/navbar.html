How it works #

Navbars require a wrapping .navbar with .navbar-expand{-sm|-md|-lg|-xl|-xxl} for responsive collapsing, and a color scheme class. The core navbar supports .navbar-brand, .navbar-nav, .navbar-toggler, and form controls.

The expansion classes configure when content should be hidden behind a navbar-toggler. Navbars and their contents are fluid by default, but you can optionally include .container to center them on a page.

Containers #

Although it’s not required, you can wrap a navbar in a .container to center it on a page or add one within to only center the contents of a fixed or static top navbar.

Responsive behaviors #

Navbars can use .navbar-toggler, .navbar-collapse, and .navbar-expand{-sm|-md|-lg|-xl|-xxl} classes to determine when their content collapses behind a button.

Toggler #

Navbars utilize toggle buttons that work with the collapse plugin. Use the .navbar-toggler-icon for the icon, and drive behavior with data-bs-toggle="collapse".
