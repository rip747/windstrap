Examples #

Toasts are lightweight notifications designed to mimic the push notifications that have been popularized by mobile and desktop operating systems.

Basic #

To encourage extensible and predictable toasts, we recommend a header and body. Toast headers use display: flex, meaning you can easily align additional content to the left or right via utility classes.
