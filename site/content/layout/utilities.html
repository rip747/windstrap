Horizontal alignment #

Bootstrap’s layout utilities let you quickly and responsively control the layout of rows and columns — centering, offsets, gutters, ordering, and more — without writing custom CSS. All of these are translated into Tailwind utilities in src/sections/layout.css and src/generated/grid.css.

What’s covered #

  • Grid layout — .container*, .row, .col*, .row-cols-*, .offset-*, .g-*/.gx-*/.gy-*
  • Flexbox — the full .d-*, .flex-*, .justify-content-*, .align-items-*, .align-content-*, .align-self-*, .order-* families
  • Spacing & sizing — .m*/.p*/.gap-*, .w-*/.h-* and responsive variants
  • Positioning — .position-*, .fixed-*, .sticky-*, .top-*, .start-*, .end-*, .translate-middle

The Position helper and the Flex and Spacing utility pages cover these in detail.
